How it works

Works alone on your machine.
Connect a server when you want company.

01 Design

Layouts that build themselves

Auto layout, constraints and grids keep every frame tidy as content changes. Components and variants carry the rules into every file.

  • Hug, fill, fixed
  • Variants and variables
  • Shared libraries
02 Prototype and motion

From static screens to a moving story

Wire up interactions, pick transitions, then open the Motion timeline to keyframe anything. Present it, share it, export it as video.

  • Overlays and scroll
  • Transitions
  • Keyframes and easing
03 Together

Everyone in the file at the same time

Live cursors, comments, version history and share links on every kind of file. Invite by email, or share a link with a key only the people you choose can open.

  • Cursors and follow
  • Comments and mentions
  • Named versions
04 Components and libraries

Design once, change everywhere

Turn anything into a component with properties and variants, publish it to a library, and every instance in every file on your server follows the change.

  • Properties and variants
  • Shared libraries
  • Instances update everywhere
