How it works

Three promises, kept by how Nagma is built rather than by a pricing page.

  1. 1

    Free forever on your machine

    Download the app and start. Your files are drafts on your computer: no account, no limits, no trial, and they work offline.

  2. 2

    Collaborate on your own server

    Run a Nagma server for your team in one command, or connect to one you already use. Live editing, comments, teams and sites, on hardware you choose.

  3. 3

    AI with your own key

    Add a key from Claude, OpenAI or a compatible server, including a model on your own machine. You pay your provider directly, if at all.

Run your own server

On a fresh Ubuntu or Debian server with a domain pointing at it. The installer sets up Docker, asks for your domain and admin email, and starts Nagma with automatic HTTPS.

One command
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fish-hook-studios/nagma/main/tools/install.sh | sudo bash

Or with Docker Compose, by hand:

Docker Compose
git clone https://github.com/fish-hook-studios/nagma.git nagma && cd nagma
cp .env.example .env   # set NAGMA_PUBLIC_URL and NAGMA_ADMINS
docker compose up -d --build

Then open the address in a browser, or add it in the app under Connect to a server. The self-hosting guide covers DNS, mail, backups and updates.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, including collaboration, teams and publishing. There's no paid tier. The hosted Nagma server is free too, and running your own costs whatever your hosting costs.

Where is my data?

On your computer, until you choose otherwise. Drafts stay on your device. Shared files live on the Nagma server you connect to, which can be your own. Nagma has no analytics or tracking built in.

How do I collaborate?

Choose Share on a file. Nagma moves it to a server and gives you a link that works anywhere. The easiest is the hosted Nagma server: free, run by the project, with an account you create in the app (verify your email before sharing). Or run your own server in one command and connect to it from the server picker. See Connect to a server and Self-hosting.

Do I need an account?

Not to use Nagma on your computer. Accounts exist only on servers, for collaboration, and each server keeps its own.

What about AI costs?

Nagma doesn't sell AI. You add a key from Claude, OpenAI or an OpenAI-compatible server, and your provider bills you for what you use. A local model costs nothing. Server admins can offer a shared key to their team if they want to. See AI keys.

Can I import my existing files?

Yes. Nagma opens design files (.fig, .sketch), slide decks (.deck, .pptx), campaign files (.buzz), page layouts (.idml), layered images (.psd), PDFs, SVG, pictures and video. The readers are clean-room implementations written for interoperability.

What's the licence?
